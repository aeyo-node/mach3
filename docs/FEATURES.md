# Features Documentation

Swaram Market Engine is equipped with extensive market intelligence features across multiple asset classes.

---

## 1. Quantitative Core Analytics
- **Indicators**: Real-time computation of SMA, EMA, RSI, MACD, ATR, and Bollinger Bands over 1-minute timeframes.
- **Fair Value Gaps (FVGs)**: Identifies unmitigated imbalance zones on price charts.
- **Order Blocks (OBs)**: Detects institutional buying/selling zones.

---

## 2. Order Flow & Depth Intelligence
- **Microprice & Imbalance**: Evaluates depth skew between bid/ask volume.
- **Liquidity Walls**: Locates large, passive orders on the L2 book.
- **Funding & Open Interest (OI) Regimes**: Detects leveraging regimes on derivatives.

---

## 3. Telemetry & Anomalies
- **Flash Crash Alert**: Flags sudden, anomalous drop in prices.
- **Spread Explosion**: Flags illiquidity events.
- **Volume Surge**: Flags institutional action.
